Coconut Braised Beef Red Curry

Beef chuck braised low and slow in coconut milk and red curry paste with lemongrass and garlic until it falls apart tender, finished with fish sauce, palm sugar, green beans and a squeeze of lime.

Pin it

Servings

6 servings

Quick Stats

2.5–3 hrs • Slow Cook • Base: 6 servings

Ingredients

  • 1kg beef chuck, cut into 3–4cm chunks, against the grain
  • 2.5 tbsp oil, for searing
  • 3.5 tbsp red curry paste
  • 800ml coconut milk (2 cans), cream skimmed off and reserved separately
  • 0.5 cup beef stock or water
  • 2.5 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 stalk lemongrass, bruised
  • 2 spring onions, sliced, optional
  • 2 tbsp fish sauce
  • 1.5 tbsp palm sugar (or brown sugar)
  • 1 lime
  • 1 handful green beans, trimmed

Method

  1. Sear the beef:
    Heat the oil in a large pot over high heat and sear the beef chunks in batches until browned on all sides. Remove and set aside.
  2. Skim the coconut cream:
    Skim the thick cream that has risen to the top of the coconut milk cans and set it aside separately from the remaining coconut liquid.
  3. Fry the curry paste:
    Fry the red curry paste in the reserved coconut cream until fragrant and the oil starts to split from the paste.
  4. Build the braising liquid:
    Add the garlic and lemongrass and cook briefly, then stir in the remaining coconut milk and the beef stock or water.
  5. Braise:
    Return the beef to the pot, cover and simmer low and slow for 2 to 2.5 hours, stirring occasionally, until fork-tender.
  6. Season and add beans:
    Stir through the fish sauce and palm sugar to taste, then add the green beans and simmer until just tender.
  7. Finish and serve:
    Remove the lemongrass, squeeze over the lime juice and scatter with spring onions, then serve with rice.

Approx Nutrition (per serve)

  • Protein: 35–40g
  • Calories: 550–650 kcal
  • High in: iron, B12 and healthy fats from coconut milk

Swaps & Additions

  • Vegetables: add carrots or potatoes in the last hour of braising alongside the green beans for a heartier dish.
  • Slow cooker: transfer to a slow cooker after searing and cook on low for 6–7 hours.
  • Heat level: add extra curry paste or fresh chilli for more spice.
  • Brightness: don't skip the lime at the end — it balances the richness of the coconut milk.
