Coconut Braised Beef Red Curry

Beef chuck braised low and slow in coconut milk and red curry paste with lemongrass and garlic until it falls apart tender, finished with fish sauce, palm sugar, green beans and a squeeze of lime.

Pin it

Servings

6 servings

Quick Stats

3.5–4 hrs • Slow Cook • Base: 6 servings

Ingredients

  • 1kg beef chuck, cut into 3–4cm chunks, against the grain
  • 2.5 tbsp oil, for searing
  • 3.5 tbsp red curry paste
  • 800ml coconut milk (2 cans), cream skimmed off and reserved separately
  • 0.5 cup beef stock or water
  • 2.5 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 onion, sliced, optional
  • 1 stalk lemongrass, bruised
  • 2 spring onions, sliced, optional
  • 2 tbsp fish sauce
  • 1.5 tbsp palm sugar (or brown sugar)
  • 1 lime
  • 1 handful green beans, trimmed
  • Rice, to serve
  • Sliced red chilli, coriander, spring onion greens, toasted peanuts or crispy shallots, to serve

Method

  1. Sear the beef:
    Sear the beef chunks in oil, in batches, without crowding the pan. Set aside.
  2. Fry the paste:
    In the same pot, add the skimmed coconut cream and curry paste. Fry until fragrant and the oil separates out.
  3. Cook the aromatics:
    Add the garlic (and onion if using), and cook until softened and lightly golden — don't let the garlic burn.
  4. Toast the lemongrass:
    Add the bruised lemongrass and toast for about a minute to release its oils.
  5. Combine:
    Return the beef to the pot, then add the remaining coconut milk and the stock.
  6. Slow cook:
    Cover and slow cook: 3 to 4 hours on low in a slow cooker, or in a covered oven at 150°C/300°F, until the beef is fork-tender.
  7. Add the beans:
    Add the green beans in the last 20 to 30 minutes of cooking.
  8. Season:
    In the same final 20 to 30 minutes, stir in the fish sauce, palm sugar and lime juice. Taste and adjust the balance of salty, sweet and sour.
  9. Serve:
    Serve over rice, topped with sliced red chilli, coriander, spring onion greens, and toasted peanuts or crispy shallots.

Approx Nutrition (per serve)

  • Protein: 35–40g
  • Calories: 550–650 kcal
  • High in: iron, B12 and healthy fats from coconut milk

Swaps & Additions

  • Vegetables: add carrots or potatoes alongside the beef for a heartier dish.
  • No slow cooker? braise covered in a pot on the stovetop over the lowest heat for the same amount of time, stirring occasionally.
  • Heat level: add extra curry paste or fresh chilli for more spice.
  • Brightness: don't skip the lime at the end — it balances the richness of the coconut milk.
