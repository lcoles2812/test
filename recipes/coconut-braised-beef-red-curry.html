Coconut Braised Beef Red Curry

Chuck steak braised low and slow in coconut milk and red curry paste with lemongrass, garlic and spring onion until it falls apart tender. A rich, warming curry that's mostly hands-off once it's in the pot.

Pin it

Servings

6 servings

Quick Stats

2.5–3 hrs • Slow Cook • Base: 6 servings

Ingredients

  • 1kg chuck steak, cut into large chunks
  • 800ml coconut milk (2 cans)
  • 3 tbsp red curry paste
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 spring onions, chopped
  • 2 lemongrass stalks, bruised
  • 500ml beef stock

Method

  1. Sear the beef:
    Heat a large pot over high heat and sear the chuck steak in batches until browned on all sides. Remove and set aside.
  2. Fry the paste:
    In the same pot, fry the red curry paste and garlic for a minute or two until fragrant.
  3. Build the braising liquid:
    Pour in the coconut milk and beef stock, then add the bruised lemongrass and spring onions. Stir to combine.
  4. Braise:
    Return the beef to the pot, cover and simmer low and slow for 2 to 2.5 hours, stirring occasionally, until the beef is fork-tender.
  5. Finish and serve:
    Remove the lemongrass stalks, taste and adjust seasoning, then serve over rice.

Approx Nutrition (per serve)

  • Protein: 35–40g
  • Calories: 550–650 kcal
  • High in: iron, B12 and healthy fats from coconut milk

Swaps & Additions

  • Vegetables: add carrots or potatoes in the last hour of braising for a heartier dish.
  • Slow cooker: transfer to a slow cooker after searing and cook on low for 6–7 hours.
  • Heat level: add extra curry paste or fresh chilli for more spice.
