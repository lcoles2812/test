High Protein Fresh Fast Slow Cook Meal Prep Dinner for Two Party Food

Beef Mince Burrito Bowl

Seared beef mince with capsicum, garlic and a simple spice blend, stirred through with black beans and corn for a proper burrito-bowl base.

Served over rice with a fresh lettuce, cucumber and tomato salad, and finished with a smoky, creamy chipotle sauce — Greek yoghurt and a little mayo mixed with chipotles in adobo.

Quick enough for a weeknight, and the mince mixture holds up well if you want to make a double batch for meal prep.

Pin it

Servings

4 servings

Quick Stats

25–30 mins • High Protein • Base: 4 servings

Ingredients

Beef Mince

  • 500g beef mince
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 capsicum, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 400g canned black beans, rinsed and drained
  • 1 cup corn kernels, rinsed if canned

To Serve

  • 2 cups rice, cooked
  • 2 cups lettuce, shredded
  • 1 Lebanese cucumber, diced
  • 1 tomato, diced

Chipotle Yoghurt Sauce

  • 2 tbsp chipotles in adobo, finely chopped, plus 1 tsp adobo sauce
  • 1 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp Greek yoghurt

Method

  1. Sear the beef mince:
    Heat a large pan over medium-high heat. Add the mince, breaking it apart, and cook until browned. Remove and set aside.
  2. Cook the onion and capsicum:
    In the same pan, cook the diced onion and capsicum until softened, about 4-5 minutes.
  3. Add garlic and spices:
    Stir in the garlic, smoked paprika, cumin, salt and pepper. Cook for 1 minute until fragrant.
  4. Return the mince:
    Add the mince back to the pan along with the black beans and corn. Stir well and cook another 3-4 minutes until heated through and combined.
  5. Cook the rice:
    While the mince cooks, prepare the rice according to packet instructions.
  6. Make the chipotle yoghurt sauce:
    Combine the chopped chipotles in adobo with a little of the adobo sauce, the mayonnaise, and the Greek yoghurt. Mix well and season to taste.
  7. Prep the salad:
    Toss together the shredded lettuce, diced cucumber, and diced tomato.
  8. Assemble the bowls:
    Divide the rice between bowls, top with the beef mince mixture and fresh salad, and finish with a generous drizzle of the chipotle yoghurt sauce.

Key Nutrients & Benefits

Beef mince and black beans bring a strong protein and iron combination, corn and fresh vegetables add fibre and vitamin C, and swapping most of the mayo for Greek yoghurt keeps the sauce creamy with a lot less fat.

Smart Swaps & Variations

  • Chicken or turkey mince instead of beef — leaner option, same method.
  • Cauliflower rice — swap the rice for a lower-carb base.
  • Extra beans, less mince — stretch the mince further and boost the fibre.
  • No mayo — use all Greek yoghurt in the sauce for an even lighter version.
  • Meal prep — the mince, rice and sauce all store well separately for 3-4 days; add the fresh salad just before eating.
