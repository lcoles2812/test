Halal Chicken Over Rice

Juicy spiced chicken thighs over fluffy rice with a hit of sriracha and a cool, creamy white sauce.

45 mins • High Protein • 5 servings

Pin it

Servings

5 servings

Quick Stats

45 mins • High Protein • Base: 5 servings

Ingredients

Chicken

  • 1kg halal chicken thighs, boneless and skinless
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tsp paprika
  • 2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 0.5 tsp black pepper

Rice and Sauces

  • 500g basmati rice
  • 250g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 1 tsp garlic salt
  • 1 tbsp mayonnaise
  • Sriracha, to serve

Method

  1. Cook the rice:
    Rinse the basmati rice, then cook according to the packet instructions. Keep covered and warm.
  2. Season the chicken:
    Toss the chicken thighs with olive oil, paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, cumin, salt and pepper.
  3. Cook the chicken:
    Heat a large pan over medium-high heat. Cook the chicken for 6-8 minutes per side, or until browned and cooked through. Rest for 5 minutes, then slice.
  4. Make the white sauce:
    Stir together Greek yoghurt, lemon juice, garlic salt and mayonnaise. Add a splash of water if needed to make it drizzleable.
  5. Serve:
    Divide rice between five bowls. Add sliced chicken, drizzle with sriracha and finish with the white sauce.

Approx Nutrition (per serve)

  • Protein: approximately 45-50g
  • Calories: approximately 650-750 kcal
  • Note: Estimates vary with the chicken cut, rice portion and sauce amounts.

Tips & Upgrades

  • Add shredded lettuce, cucumber or tomato for freshness.
  • Use extra sriracha for more heat, or keep it on the side for serving.
  • For meal prep, store the sauces separately and reheat the chicken and rice before assembling.
