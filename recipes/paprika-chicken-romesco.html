Paprika Chicken with Romesco & Broccolini

Garlic and paprika marinated chicken served with a bold romesco sauce made from roasted capsicum, tomato, garlic and pumpkin seeds.

Pin it

It is fresh, smoky and high-protein, with charred broccolini for a simple veg side. Great for a single-night dinner that still feels a little special.

Servings

4 servings

Quick Stats

35–45 mins • High Protein • Base: 4 servings

Ingredients

Garlic Paprika Chicken

  • 800g chicken thighs (boneless, skinless)
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp sweet paprika
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 lemon (juice)
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 0.5 tsp black pepper

Romesco Sauce

  • 2 red capsicums
  • 1 ripe tomato
  • 4 cloves garlic (roasted)
  • 0.5 cup pumpkin seeds, toasted
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp red wine vinegar
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 0.5 tsp chilli flakes (optional)
  • 0.75 tsp salt

Broccolini

  • 2 bunches broccolini
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 0.5 tsp garlic powder
  • 0.5 lemon (to finish)

Method

  1. Marinate the chicken:
    Mix olive oil, garlic, smoked paprika, sweet paprika, cumin, lemon juice, salt and pepper. Coat chicken and marinate for at least 15 minutes.
  2. Roast the romesco veg:
    Roast capsicums, tomato and garlic at 220°C fan-forced for 20-25 minutes until softened and lightly charred. Peel most of the charred skin from capsicums if you like a smoother sauce.
  3. Blend romesco:
    Add roasted capsicum, tomato, garlic, pumpkin seeds, olive oil, vinegar, smoked paprika, chilli flakes and salt to a blender. Blend to a thick sauce. Add a splash of water if needed to loosen.
  4. Cook the chicken:
    Heat a large pan over medium-high heat. Cook chicken 5-7 minutes each side until caramelised and cooked through, then rest briefly and slice.
  5. Char broccolini:
    Toss broccolini with olive oil, garlic powder and salt. Roast or pan-char 8-10 minutes until tender-crisp. Finish with lemon juice.
  6. Serve:
    Spoon romesco onto the plate, add sliced paprika chicken and broccolini, and finish with extra pumpkin seeds if you like.

Approx Nutrition (per serve)

  • Protein: 42-48g
  • Calories: 540-650 kcal
  • High in: vitamin C, vitamin E, iron and healthy fats

Swaps & Additions

  • No blender: Finely chop romesco ingredients and mix with olive oil and vinegar for a rustic sauce.
  • Nutty option: Swap pumpkin seeds for almonds if preferred.
  • Extra carbs: Serve with couscous, rice, or roasted potatoes.
  • Spice level: Add more chilli flakes or a little hot sauce to the romesco.
