Thai Red Chicken Curry

A quick, creamy Thai red curry with tender chicken thighs, broccoli and capsicum, finished with Thai basil, coriander and fresh chilli.

The key is frying the curry paste before the coconut milk goes in. It only takes a minute or two, but it wakes up the spices and gives the sauce its deep, rounded flavour.

It's on the table in about half an hour, so it's an easy one for a busy weeknight. Serve it with jasmine rice to soak up the sauce.

Pin it

Servings

3 servings

Quick Stats

30–35 mins • Weeknight • Base: 3 servings

Ingredients

Curry

  • 600g chicken thighs, sliced
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil
  • 2 tbsp Thai red curry paste (adjust to heat)
  • 400ml coconut milk
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 red capsicum, sliced
  • 1 head of broccoli, cut into small florets
  • 1 tbsp fish sauce
  • 1 tsp brown or palm sugar (optional)

To Serve

  • 1 small handful Thai basil leaves
  • 0.25 cups fresh coriander, chopped
  • 1 green chilli, thinly sliced

Method

  1. Sear the chicken:
    Heat the oil in a large pan over medium-high heat. Add the chicken and brown lightly, about 4-5 minutes. This builds flavour before the sauce goes in. Remove and set aside.
  2. Fry the curry paste:
    Reduce the heat to medium and fry the red curry paste in the same pan for 1-2 minutes, stirring, until fragrant. This blooms the spices and releases their aromatic oils.
  3. Build the sauce:
    Slowly pour in the coconut milk, stirring until the paste fully dissolves into a smooth, rich sauce. Bring to a gentle simmer.
  4. Simmer the vegetables:
    Add the garlic, capsicum and broccoli. Simmer gently for 4-5 minutes so the broccoli softens slightly but stays bright green with a bit of bite.
  5. Return the chicken:
    Add the chicken back to the pan with any juices. Simmer for 5-8 minutes until it's cooked through and the sauce has thickened slightly.
  6. Balance the flavour:
    Stir in the fish sauce for saltiness and depth. Taste, and add the sugar only if it needs balancing against the heat and richness. The sauce should be glossy and cling to the chicken; add a splash of water if it's too thick.
  7. Finish and serve:
    Take the pan off the heat. Serve over jasmine rice, topped with Thai basil, coriander and sliced green chilli.

Key Nutrients & Benefits

Chicken thighs bring plenty of protein for muscle repair, and coconut milk provides fats for energy. Broccoli and capsicum add fibre, vitamin C and vitamin K, and the fresh herbs and chilli finish it with a bright, aromatic lift.

Approx. calories: about 700 kcal per serve

Smart Swaps & Variations

  • Different veg: swap the broccoli for green beans, snow peas, zucchini or baby corn, or add a handful of spinach at the end.
  • Prawn version: use 500g peeled prawns instead of chicken and add them in the last 3-4 minutes so they stay tender.
  • Tofu version: use firm tofu, pan-fried until golden, and soy sauce in place of fish sauce for a vegetarian curry.
  • Lighter version: use light coconut milk and add a splash of chicken stock to keep the sauce loose.
  • Turn the heat up or down: add more curry paste and keep the chilli seeds for extra heat, or use less paste and skip the fresh chilli for a milder curry.
